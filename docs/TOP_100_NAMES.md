# Atlas — 100 Candidate Names

Generated across the seven categories the brief names, then put through two
pass/fail gates before scoring.

**The agency gate** — does *"{Name} projects 52.3 against a market of 53.5"*
read as English? `BRAND_GUIDE.md` defines the verbs the brand must take, and
the name is their subject 76+ times in shipped copy. **A** = passes.

**The category gate** — does it share a register with gambling (action, edge,
sharp, lock, play, odds, book, line, juice, pick)? **X** = eliminated.

## Gate results

| | Count |
|---|---|
| Generated | 100 |
| **Eliminated by the category gate** | **14** |
| **Failed the agency gate** | 69 |
| **Passed both** | **31** |

The agency gate did most of the work, and it was the right filter: it is the
only criterion derived from something already true of the product rather than
from taste. A name that cannot be the subject of *"… marks its own card down"*
would force a rewrite of the one sentence the whole brand rests on.

The 31 survivors carry into `TOP_25_NAMES.md`, where six are cut on
defensibility and scoring begins.
