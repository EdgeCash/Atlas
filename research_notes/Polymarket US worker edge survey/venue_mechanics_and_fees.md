## Key question 1: Fee schedule (who pays, formula, category differences, tiers, rebates)

### Takeaway
Polymarket US charges a taker-only fee of Fee = 0.0695 × C × p × (1 − p) (0.10 for table tennis), identical across all categories as of Oct 24, 2026; makers pay zero and receive a rebate only if they did at least $10M of maker volume in the prior month (so a $135 account gets no rebate on either side). The modelled formula (coefficient × price × (1 − price) per contract) is correct and the 0.0695 coefficient is confirmed by both the official fee page and all 22,192 open markets on the gateway.

### Cited Findings
- Official formula: "Fee = Θ × C × p × (1 - p)" where C is contracts, p is trade price ($0.01–$0.99), Θ the fee coefficient; Taker Fee Θ = 0.0695 (max $1.74 per 100 contracts at p = 0.50); Taker Fee (Table Tennis) Θ = 0.10; Maker Rebate Θ = −0.0035 to −0.0104 by maker tier. Schedule "Effective exchange-wide from 1:00 PM ET, Monday, October 19, 2026." — [Fee Schedule, docs.polymarket.us](https://docs.polymarket.us/fees.md)
- Maker rebate tiers (prior-calendar-month maker volume): Tier 1 below $10,000,000 = no maker rebate; Tier 2 $10M–$49.99M = −0.0035; Tier 3 $50M–$199.99M = −0.0070; Tier 4 $200M+ = −0.0104. "Maker rebates use the same tier coefficient in every market, including Table Tennis and crypto." Maker rebate is credited at the point of trade. — [Fee Schedule](https://docs.polymarket.us/fees.md)
- Taker rebate tiers (prior-month taker volume): below $250,000 none; $250K–$999,999 3%; $1M–$9.99M 5%; $10M–$24.99M 10%; $25M–$49.99M 15%; $50M+ 30% of taker fees paid; paid weekly. — [Fee Schedule](https://docs.polymarket.us/fees.md)
- Volume for tiers is cash paid for the side received, not face value: Volume(buy) = C × p; Volume(sell) = C × (1 − p); combo fills count toward both; voided fills excluded; measured in ET calendar months. — [Fee Schedule](https://docs.polymarket.us/fees.md)
- Rounding: "All fees and rebates are rounded to the nearest $0.01 using banker's rounding"; multi-fill aggressive orders are charged per fill but capped so the total never exceeds the rounded cumulative exact fee; "On small trades (low quantity or prices near $0.00 or $1.00), the fee can round down to $0.00"; no fee on canceled, expired, or rejected orders; taker fees deducted at trade time. — [Fee Schedule](https://docs.polymarket.us/fees.md)
- Combo (multi-leg) taker fee uses a separate curve: Fee = C × p × [0.0695 × (1 − p) + 0.06 × (1 − p)^4]; combo fills carry no maker rebate. — [Fee Schedule](https://docs.polymarket.us/fees.md)
- Before Oct 19, 2026 the maker rebate was a flat −0.0125 for everyone; an additional 17% weekly crypto maker rebate ended 8:00 AM ET Oct 15, 2026; "Taker fees are unchanged" by the Oct 19 change. — [Changelog, docs.polymarket.us](https://docs.polymarket.us/changelog.md)
- Table tennis taker coefficient raised from 0.0695 to 0.10 effective 12:00 AM ET Oct 7, 2026; the notice states this "does not amend the taker fee coefficient for other straight trades, maker rebates, taker rebate tiers or block trade fees." — [Exchange Notice, Weekly Notification of Fee Schedule Change, 2026-10-13 (PDF)](https://polymarketexchange.com/files/notices/Exchange%20Notice%20-%20Weekly%20Notification%20of%20Fee%20Schedule%20Change%20(2026.10.13).pdf)
- Pending change (CFTC self-certification PMX-2026-64, dated Oct 23, 2026, effective Nov 6, 2026): rebate tiers "will be set daily based on trailing 30-day notional volume" instead of prior calendar month; for days 8–30 after a participant's first trade, the tier is the higher of trailing-30-day volume or first-7-day volume × 30/7; "Tier thresholds, rebate percentages and maker rebate coefficients are unchanged"; "The maker fee is zero." Accelerated Tier (verifiable volume on another prediction market) limited to 30 days. — [Exchange Notice, Amendment to Taker and Maker Rebate Tiers, 2026-10-23 (PDF)](https://polymarketexchange.com/files/notices/Exchange%20Notice%20-%20Amendment%20to%20Taker%20and%20Maker%20Rebate%20Tiers%20(2026.10.23).pdf)
- Gateway confirmation (read-only scan 2026-10-24, /v1/events?closed=false, 22,192 open markets): feeCoefficient = 0.0695 on every market in every category (sports 13,214; politics 6,807; culture 1,698; macro 202; crypto 82; finance 78; technology 69; geopolitics 17; science 14; climate 11). No table-tennis market was open at scan time, so the 0.10 coefficient was not observed. — [gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events?closed=false&limit=500&offset=0)
- Rulebook: the Company sets fees and "may post an updated fee schedule on its website from time to time. Participants are deemed to have notice of any changes"; fees are withdrawn directly from the Participant's Account. — [Polymarket US Rulebook, March 20, 2026, Rule 3.x fees (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03202641475.pdf)
- API: execution reports carry `aggressor` (true = taker) and `commissionNotionalCollected`; order preview returns `commissionsBasisPoints`, `makerCommissionsBasisPoints`, and `commissionNotionalTotalCollected`. — [Create Order](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/orders/create-order.md), [Preview Order](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/orders/preview-order.md)
- CONFLICTING secondary claims (not corroborated by official docs or gateway data): River Markets (updated Aug 9, 2026) describes a category-based US taker schedule effective July 1, 2026 of 0% geopolitics / 4% politics-finance-tech / 5% sports / 7% crypto — [River Markets](https://www.rivermarkets.com/insights/polymarket-us-fees.html); Sacra describes a 0.05 taker fee with −0.0125 maker rebate effective April 3, 2026 — [Sacra](https://www.sacra.com/c/polymarket/); DeFi Rate cites a 0.10% taker fee and inGame cited a flat 0.01% at the Nov 2025 launch — [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/prediction-markets/fees/), [inGame](https://www.ingame.com/?p=36373). The River category rates match the GLOBAL venue's Fee Structure V2 (effective March 30, 2026) more than anything on docs.polymarket.us.
- GLOBAL (for contrast, do not apply to US): the global CLOB docs say "makers are never charged fees," only takers pay, and rates vary by category — [docs.polymarket.com fees](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees).

### Inferences
- For a pure taker bot buying at price p, the fee as a fraction of cash outlay is Θ(1 − p): 0.70% at p = 0.90, 1.4% at 0.80, 3.5% at 0.50, 5.6% at 0.20. A $25 IOC buy at 0.90 (27.78 contracts) costs 0.0695 × 27.78 × 0.09 = $0.174 → $0.17; the same $25 at 0.50 (50 contracts) costs $0.87. Fees on $1 orders at prices ≥0.90 round to $0.00 (0.0695 × 1.1 × 0.09 = $0.007).
- Because the rebate only starts at $10M/month maker volume, a small account should treat maker fee = 0 and taker fee = full 0.0695 curve; there is no fee reason to prefer resting orders beyond avoiding the taker fee itself.
- The venue changes fee parameters by weekly 40.6(d) notification; the bot should read `feeCoefficient` per market from the gateway rather than hard-code 0.0695 (table tennis is already 0.10).

### Gaps
- Block-trade fee schedule referenced in the Oct 13 notice was not retrieved.
- The exact previous launch-era schedules (0.01% / 0.10% reported Nov 2025 – early 2026) could not be verified against primary documents; treat as historical and superseded.

## Key question 2: Order types, time-in-force, tick sizes, minimum size, limits, rate limits

### Takeaway
The Retail API supports limit and market orders with DAY/GTC/GTD/IOC/FOK time-in-force, a post-only flag (`participateDontInitiate`), market-order slippage tolerance, batched create/cancel/modify, and decimal (0.01-contract) quantities on most markets; tick size is per instrument (0.01 on ~94% of open markets, 0.005/0.0025/0.001 on others) and the public limit is 25 requests/s per source IP.

### Cited Findings
- Order types: `ORDER_TYPE_LIMIT` (price required) and `ORDER_TYPE_MARKET`; time in force: `TIME_IN_FORCE_DAY`, `GOOD_TILL_CANCEL`, `GOOD_TILL_DATE` (with `goodTillTime`), `IMMEDIATE_OR_CANCEL`, `FILL_OR_KILL`. `participateDontInitiate`: "If true, order must rest on the book prior to matching (maker only). Order will be rejected if it would immediately match." Market orders may be sized in cash via `cashOrderQty` and carry `slippageTolerance` in `bips` or `ticks` relative to `currentPrice`. `synchronousExecution` + `maxBlockTime` block until fill/reject. `manualOrderIndicator` = MANUAL or AUTOMATIC "placed ... automatically by a trading system". Intent enums BUY_LONG/SELL_LONG/BUY_SHORT/SELL_SHORT or `outcomeSide` + `action`. All endpoints require Ed25519 signature auth (X-PM-Access-Key, X-PM-Timestamp, X-PM-Signature). — [Create Order (OpenAPI)](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/orders/create-order.md)
- Batched endpoints: `POST /v1/orders/batched`, `/v1/orders/batched/cancel`, `/v1/orders/batched/modify`. — [Changelog](https://docs.polymarket.us/changelog.md)
- Single instrument per market (YES side only); selling YES = buying NO; app-level expiry choices are GTC, 12am PT, Start of game, or IOC; matching is "standard time-and-price priority within the central limit order book". — [Orders & Trading](https://docs.polymarket.us/concepts/orders.md), [Order Types](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/trading/basics/order-types.md)
- Fractional contracts: "Most markets ... trade in steps of 0.01 contracts (1% of a contract). A small number of markets, mostly futures, still trade in whole contracts only." All newly listed instruments became partial-contract markets on June 11, 2026 at 5:00 PM ET. `minimumTradeQty` is in contracts (0.01 = 1% of a contract); `orderPriceMinTickSize` is in dollars. — [Fractional Contracts](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/trading/basics/fractional-shares.md), [Changelog](https://docs.polymarket.us/changelog.md)
- Tick sizes observed on the gateway 2026-10-24 (open markets): 0.01 on 21,128; 0.001 on 872 (739 sports futures, 133 politics); 0.005 on 164 sports; 0.0025 on 28 sports. minimumTradeQty: 0.01 on 21,612; 1 on 580 (mostly sports and finance futures). Example: MLB World Series futures quote to 4 decimals (0.7200/0.7210) with tick 0.001. — [gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events?closed=false&limit=500&offset=0)
- Decimalization history: NBA Finals Game 2 (June 1, 2026) was the first 0.5¢-tick market; World Cup spreads/totals and Wimbledon match winners use 0.5¢; "Do not assume 1 cent ticks. Read tick size ... per market." — [Changelog](https://docs.polymarket.us/changelog.md)
- App-level limit: "a single market buy is limited to $1,000,000, and combos have their own maximum stake." — [Trading Limits](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/trading/access-and-limits/trading-limits.md)
- Position limits: "Position limits may be established by the Company from time to time for any Contract. The maximum position will be set forth in the Contract Rules of any Contract with such limits"; accounts are aggregated per CFTC Reg. 150.4; position accountability lets the exchange demand information and order a position not be increased. Rule 5.3(h): "The Company may establish a maximum Order size for the Account of each Participant based upon the Participant's available funds, the Participant's trading behavior, and..." — [Rulebook 5.9 and 5.3 (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03202641475.pdf)
- Rate limits (Retail API): "general limit of 25 requests/s, shared across endpoints except quote creation and deletion"; "enforced per source IP and Cloudflare location, so requests using different API keys from the same IP share counters"; exceptions: POST /v1/combos and POST /v1/rfqs 1 req/s shared; POST /v1/rfqs/quotes 100 req/s; GET /v1/rfqs* 1 req/s. 429 → wait ≥1 s, exponential backoff. Higher production limits: email support@polymarket.us with endpoints and expected volume. — [Rate Limits](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/rate-limits.md)
- Latency stopgap: "During periods of increased latency, Polymarket US applies a 5-second stopgap to inbound orders. If an order has been received ... but has not been processed within 5 seconds, we reject it"; these rejects say "Global Rate Limit Exceeded" but "are not an actual rate limit"; applies to new orders and cancel/replace, never to pure cancels. — [Rate Limits](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/rate-limits.md)
- Observed 2026-10-24: 30 consecutive GET /v1/markets/{slug}/bbo calls on the public gateway completed in 3.8 s with no 429. — [gateway BBO](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/markets/aec-boxing-canalv-chrmbi-2026-10-31-canalv-chrmbi/bbo)
- Earlier changelog entry (older, superseded by the page above): "Retail API: reduced to 20 requests per second per API key". — [Changelog](https://docs.polymarket.us/changelog.md)
- Institutional API (separate, firm-level): GetBBO and GetOrderBook limited to 12 req/min per firm; FIX order-entry 35–500 msg/s per session by tier; 20 concurrent gRPC streams per firm; 100 ingress msg/s. — [Trader Guide Rate Limits](https://docs.polymarket.us/trader-guide/rate-limits.md)
- Collateral: contracts are fully collateralized; buyers pay price, sellers post $1.00 per contract; open orders consume buying power per instrument (worst case within the instrument only), and a fill elsewhere can trigger automatic cancellation of resting orders that become unfunded; "Executed trades are never unwound for collateral reasons." — [Collateral and Margin](https://docs.polymarket.us/market-structure/collateral-and-margin.md)
- Rulebook 5.3(d): entering orders without sufficient funds is prohibited and may be penalized; 5.3(b): an order rate limiter may cap orders per second. — [Rulebook (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03202641475.pdf)
- Maintenance: weekly window Thursday 6:00–8:00 AM ET (effective Sept 24, 2026; previously 2–4 AM ET and before July 9, 2026 6–8 AM ET); "All open orders are canceled before maintenance begins"; API returns 503 during maintenance; books reopen empty. Trade day ends 5:00 PM ET. — [General FAQs](https://docs.polymarket.us/faqs/general-faqs.md), [Trading Hours](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/trading/access-and-limits/trading-hours.md)

### Inferences
- The modelled "IOC limit at the ask" strategy is fully supported; `participateDontInitiate` enables a true post-only maker mode if a resting strategy is tried, and FOK avoids partial fills on thin top-of-book.
- At 45-second polling the bot is two orders of magnitude under the 25 req/s cap; the real constraint is that per-IP counting means any other process on the same VPS IP shares the budget.
- Rule 5.2(i) (see Q5) requires throttles, price collars and kill switches for automated systems; `slippageTolerance` and preview-before-send satisfy the "price collar" element cheaply.

### Gaps
- Per-contract position limits live in individual Contract Rules (product self-certifications) and were not retrieved; no evidence of a position limit binding at $135 scale.
- No published figure for a per-account maximum order size under Rule 5.3(h).

## Key question 3: Streaming feeds and price-feed latency

### Takeaway
There is an authenticated WebSocket (wss://api.polymarket.us/v1/ws/markets) with full book, lite BBO and trade streams; the public REST gateway is edge-cached with Cache-Control max-age=30, so a 45-second poll can be reading a BBO that is up to ~30 s stale relative to the matching engine.

### Cited Findings
- WebSocket endpoints: `wss://api.polymarket.us/v1/ws/private` (ORDER=1, POSITION=3, ACCOUNT_BALANCE=4) and `wss://api.polymarket.us/v1/ws/markets` (MARKET_DATA=1 full book and stats, MARKET_DATA_LITE=2 BBO/depth counts, TRADE=3); both require API key headers in the handshake, signature over `timestamp + "GET" + path`; max 100 markets per subscription; optional `responsesDebounced`; heartbeats sent by server. — [WebSocket Overview](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/websocket/overview.md), [Markets WebSocket](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/websocket/markets.md)
- Docs explicitly recommend replacing `GET /v1/markets/{slug}/bbo` polling with MARKET_DATA_LITE and `/book` polling with MARKET_DATA. — [Rate Limits](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/rate-limits.md)
- Public base URLs: authenticated `https://api.polymarket.us`, public market data `https://gateway.polymarket.us` (no key). — [API Introduction](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/introduction.md)
- Observed 2026-10-24 on the public gateway: `GET /v1/markets/{slug}/bbo` returned `Cache-Control: public, max-age=30` with `CF-Cache-Status: HIT` and `Age` climbing 3→29 across 11 calls while the embedded `lastPriceSample.ts` stayed fixed at 23:39:52Z; the 12th call was `EXPIRED` and the sample advanced to 23:40:38Z. `/book` also HIT with Age 18; `/v1/events` EXPIRED; `/v1/price-history` MISS. Round-trip latency 0.12–0.19 s from a US-East Cloudflare POP (CF-RAY ...-IAD). — [gateway BBO](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/markets/aec-boxing-canalv-chrmbi-2026-10-31-canalv-chrmbi/bbo)
- BBO payload fields: currentPx, lastTradePx, settlementPx, sharesTraded, openInterest, bestBid/bestAsk, bidDepth/askDepth (number of levels), bidShares/askShares, lastPriceSample{longPx, shortPx, ts}, state. — [gateway BBO](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/markets/aec-boxing-canalv-chrmbi-2026-10-31-canalv-chrmbi/bbo)
- Price history endpoint returns one-minute `longPrice`/`shortPrice` samples (`fidelity=1`). — [gateway price-history](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/price-history)
- Institutional streaming (gRPC/HTTP streaming, separate product): at-least-once delivery, snapshot-then-delta for order and market data, 20 concurrent streams per firm. — [Streaming](https://docs.polymarket.us/trader-guide/streaming-apis.md)
- Live sports events on the gateway expose `live`, `period`, and score metadata. — [Events & Markets](https://docs.polymarket.us/concepts/events-and-markets.md)

### Inferences
- With a 30 s edge cache plus a 45 s poll, the bot's view of the ask can lag the engine by 0–75 s; in live sports that is enough for the ask to have moved several cents, which is consistent with "resting bids under the market lose 4–10 points" and with IOC fills at a stale ask being adversely selected. Using the authenticated WebSocket (same Ed25519 key) removes the cache layer.
- Matching-engine latency is not published; the 5-second stopgap implies order processing can exceed 5 s under load.

### Gaps
- No official statement of engine-to-WebSocket or engine-to-gateway latency; the 30 s figure is an observed cache TTL on one day and could change.
- Whether `api.polymarket.us` REST BBO (authenticated) is also cached was not tested (no authenticated calls made).

## Key question 4: Settlement rules and timing

### Takeaway
Sports contracts settle to the official governing-body result, "often shortly after the official result is posted," with ties at $0.50 (winner markets), postponements carried to the rescheduled game if within the ~two-week expiration, and cancellations/no-contests settled at "last fair market price" as of the announcement; crypto settles on CF Benchmarks BRTI 60-second averages within about a minute (Up/Down) to 15 minutes (ladders); politics/news settle on named official sources and can take longer.

### Cited Findings
- Resolution: markets resolve using "the specific sources listed in the rules"; "Sports markets: Often resolve shortly after the official result is posted"; "Political or news markets: May take longer if certification or official publication is required"; winning contracts settle at $1.00, losing at $0.00; Polymarket Clearing processes settlement; "All settlements are final in accordance with the Polymarket US Exchange Rulebook"; some markets have "Alternative Settlement" terms. — [Market Resolution](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/markets/market-resolution.md), [Market Settlement](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/markets/contract-settlement.md)
- Sports source hierarchy: primary = governing body; secondary = official scorecards, referee reports, league databases; tertiary = AP, Reuters, ESPN, BBC Sport, team/league sites. "Settlement is delayed if the official result is under review. If no official result is declared by the Contract's expiration date, the Contract settles at last fair market prices." — [Sports FAQs](https://docs.polymarket.us/faqs/sports-faqs.md)
- Last fair market price (LFMP): "the prevailing fair market price on the Exchange at a specified moment in time, typically the moment an official announcement is made ... It is not the last traded price at market close. Markets may remain open for a period after an announcement, and trades that occur during this window are executed at the trader's own risk." — [Sports FAQs](https://docs.polymarket.us/faqs/sports-faqs.md)
- Ties: Winner (w/o tie) settles at $0.50 on a tie; spreads/totals use half points; co-winners split $1.00 rounded down to the tick. Overtime/shootouts included except soccer league matches (regulation time only). — [Sports FAQs](https://docs.polymarket.us/faqs/sports-faqs.md)
- Postponement: if rescheduled before expiration "(typically two weeks from the original event date)" the rescheduled result governs; otherwise LFMP; soccer home/away flip → LFMP. Suspended/abandoned games: official threshold reached → result stands; not reached and not completed before expiration → LFMP. Cancelled and never replayed → LFMP as of the announcement. Pre-event withdrawal → LFMP for game-level contracts; futures/qualifiers of a withdrawn participant → $0.00. Forfeit: awarded side wins. No contest → LFMP. Venue change: no effect. — [Sports FAQs](https://docs.polymarket.us/faqs/sports-faqs.md)
- Futures settle on the official champion declaration; a team's contract "may settle at $0.00 as soon as it is mathematically impossible for them to win ... and that elimination has been officially recognized." Gateway example rule text (MLB World Series futures): postponed → expiration moved; not rescheduled within two weeks → may settle at LFMP; eliminated → $0.00. — [Sports FAQs](https://docs.polymarket.us/faqs/sports-faqs.md), [gateway event mlb-champ-2026-09-27](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events?slug=mlb-champ-2026-09-27)
- Gateway moneyline rule text (boxing, Oct 2026): "If this match is postponed or delayed, the market will remain open and settle following the conclusion of the rescheduled match. If the match is cancelled, the market will settle to last fair market price." — [gateway market](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/markets?slug=aec-boxing-canalv-chrmbi-2026-10-31-canalv-chrmbi)
- Gateway settled examples: NFL moneylines for Nov 2, 2025 games show status MARKET_STATUS_RESOLVED, `endDate` 24 h after `gameStartTime`, and `GET /v1/markets/{slug}/settlement` returns `{"settlement": 1}` (or 0); the endpoint 404s for unsettled markets. — [gateway settlement](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/markets/aec-nfl-car-gb-2025-11-02/settlement)
- Crypto: all markets settle on CF Benchmarks' BRTI (published once per second); Up/Down, Above/Below and Price Range use the simple average of the 60 prices in [T − 60 s, T); One-Touch uses a rolling 60-s 20% trimmed mean and can settle early; ties: Up/Down flat → Up, equal to strike → Yes; "Up/Down windows settle shortly after the window closes, typically within a minute. Above/Below and Price Range ladders settle within about fifteen minutes of expiry"; expiration set 30 min after the settlement instant, trading ends at the instant; settlement deferred if index data incomplete. 15-minute windows listed ~12 h ahead, open for trading ~90 s before start. — [Crypto FAQs](https://docs.polymarket.us/faqs/crypto-faqs.md)
- Metals: gold/silver settle on Chainlink XAU/USD and XAG/USD spot streams at window start/end; manual settlement if price missing one hour after close. — [Market Resolution](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/markets/market-resolution.md)
- Politics/culture/macro/science examples from gateway rules: House control sourced from state electoral authorities and the US House (endDate 2027-02-02); Spotify #1 artist verified by Spotify; Fed cut sourced from the Federal Reserve; measles count from CDC; hottest year from NASA GISS "as first published ... Subsequent revisions will not be considered." — [gateway events](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events?closed=false&limit=500&offset=0)

- A CFTC product-terms template for event contracts likewise provides LFMP or expiration extension on cancellation and amended settlement date on postponement. — [CFTC product filing (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/ptc/ptc0531264663.pdf)
- GLOBAL contrast: archived global Polymarket MLB markets resolve 50-50 if a game is cancelled with no make-up — different from the US LFMP rule. — [polyguana archive of global market](https://polyguana.com/market/1930125)

### Inferences
- "Shortly after the official result" is not quantified; capital in a winning sports position is unavailable until Polymarket Clearing posts settlement, so same-night recycling of a $135 bankroll across multiple games cannot be assumed.
- LFMP settlement on cancellations means a heavy-favorite position bought at 0.95 is marked at the pre-announcement fair price, not $1, so cancellation risk is a small but real haircut on favorites.

### Gaps
- No official number of minutes between final whistle and credited settlement; needs empirical measurement via the private WebSocket POSITION/ACCOUNT_BALANCE stream.
- Who exactly determines LFMP (methodology) is not published beyond "prevailing fair market price on the Exchange."

## Key question 5: Terms of service and compliance for bots

### Takeaway
API/automated trading is explicitly contemplated and permitted (the API has a MANUAL/AUTOMATIC order flag and a documented path to higher limits "for automated systems"), but the Participant Agreement requires conformance testing and written approval before live API use, and the Rulebook requires pre-trade throttles, price collars and kill switches, bans self-matching across commonly controlled accounts "whether Orders are entered directly, via API, or through an FCM," and bans spoofing, message flooding, and trading on confidential or outcome-influencing information; no rule specifically prohibits latency arbitrage.

### Cited Findings
- "Does Polymarket have an API? Yes. Polymarket US provides a Retail API with documentation for market data, orders, portfolio, and related endpoints, plus Python and TypeScript SDKs." — [API FAQ](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/faq/api.md)
- Rate-limit page section "For automated systems: If you're running an automated trading system and need higher limits for production: Document your use case ... Email support@polymarket.us." — [Rate Limits](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/rate-limits.md)
- Participant Agreement §19: "You shall comply with all conformance tests and procedures required by Polymarket US when connecting to the System via API. You shall not access or use the System in a live production environment unless and until you have received written approval to do so from Polymarket US. You shall be responsible and liable for any errors or failure in your implementation of the API." §18: participant must keep connected systems secure and allow PMUS to audit system security on reasonable notice. — [Individual Participant and Clearing Member Agreement (PDF)](https://polymarketexchange.com/files/legal/latest/participant-agreement)
- Participant Agreement representations: natural person ≥18; not domiciled, resident of, or located in a jurisdiction where trading is prohibited "under Applicable Law or by PMUS policy," including OFAC-sanctioned regions and FATF black/grey-list jurisdictions; governed by New York law; the Rules govern over the Agreement in a conflict. — [Participant Agreement (PDF)](https://polymarketexchange.com/files/legal/latest/participant-agreement)
- Rulebook 5.2(h)-(i): "Participants shall maintain an electronic audit trail capturing order entry, modification, cancellation, and execution timestamps"; "Participants utilizing automated trading systems must implement pre-trade risk controls including order throttles, price collars, and kill switches." 5.3(c): any participant submitting orders "whether manually or via automated functionality, must ensure adequate controls are in place to prevent excessive messaging." 5.3(g): "Any Person receiving information through the Trading System is expressly prohibited from redistributing such information unless expressly permitted." — [Rulebook (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03202641475.pdf)
- Rulebook 7.2 Prohibited Practices: disruptive trading incl. spoofing, submitting/cancelling "with the intent to overload the Trading System," "to delay another person's execution," or to create "false market depth" (e); collusion to move prices (f); trading on confidential information in breach of a duty, on tips, or when holding influence over the outcome (g)(h)(i); trading on nonpublic information of another person's impending transaction (j); wash sales (p); self-matching: "A Participant ... must not intentionally match, directly or indirectly, an Order it has placed ... in any other capacity or account ... This prohibition applies across all accounts under common ownership, control, or direction, whether Orders are entered directly, via API, or through an FCM Participant or Broker Participant" (q); structuring activity to evade surveillance (m); pre-arranged trades (s). — [Rulebook (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03202641475.pdf)
- Multiple accounts: a Customer "may maintain accounts with multiple FCM Participants or Broker Participants, provided the Customer does not intentionally match its own orders across such accounts" and must disclose each account to the Company (Rule 3.2(e)). — [Rulebook (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03202641475.pdf)
- Market Integrity policy (integrity.polymarket.us): three rules — no trading on stolen confidential information, on illegal tips, or if you can influence the outcome; scenarios include team staff, campaign staff, CEOs on "Mentions" markets. Some contracts additionally bar anyone with MNPI from the resolution source or connected to a sports event (players, coaches, staff, owners, household members). — [Polymarket US Market Integrity](https://integrity.polymarket.us/), [Trading Restrictions](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/trading/access-and-limits/trading-restrictions.md)
- KYC: sign-up requires date of birth, phone, legal name, residential address and Social Security number; may require government ID and selfie; "You must complete KYC before you can deposit funds or trade." — [Create an Account](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/get-started/signup.md)
- US vs global are separate venues: "Polymarket is our international, crypto-based product that operates on blockchain technology. Polymarket US is a fiat-based, US-regulated platform operating as a designated contract market (DCM) and derivatives clearing organization (DCO) ... All trading is conducted in US dollars." — [What is Polymarket US?](https://docs.polymarket.us/getting-started/what-is-polymarket-us.md); secondary: the two "do not share accounts, order books, fees or prices" — [ThinBlimp](https://thinblimp.com/guides/polymarket-us-vs-polymarket)
- Eligibility: Rulebook 3.1(c) requires individual participants to be ≥18 and not statutorily disqualified; the Company may deny or condition Trading Privileges in its sole discretion. — [Rulebook (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03202641475.pdf)
- States (secondary only): Action Network's review (updated Sept 2026) lists about 40 eligible jurisdictions including DC, 18+ — [Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/prediction-markets/polymarket); pm.wiki says US ACH opened in 40+ states after the waitlist was removed in May 2026 — [pm.wiki](https://pm.wiki/es/learn/polymarket-withdrawal-guide). No official state list was found on docs.polymarket.us; the Participant Agreement bars only sanctioned/FATF jurisdictions by name and otherwise defers to "PMUS policy."
- Error/ clearly erroneous trade policy: the Rulebook text retrieved contains no dedicated error-trade or trade-bust rule beyond invalidating trades of suspended intermediaries' customers (5.11). — [Rulebook (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03202641475.pdf)

### Inferences
- A retail account that obtained API keys through the app and has been trading live is presumably already "approved" in the §19 sense, but the clause gives the exchange grounds to suspend API access for bot errors; keeping local order/cancel logs with timestamps (5.2(h)) and a kill switch (5.2(i)) is both required and cheap.
- Operating a second account (or a friend's) to provide liquidity to oneself would violate 7.2(q) even if unintentional matches occur; the MM program surveillance explicitly flags "trading between related or coordinated accounts."
- Nothing found prohibits polling public data and taking stale quotes (latency arbitrage against slow makers); the exchange's own 5-second stopgap is designed to protect takers, not makers.

### Gaps
- The polymarket.us/tos page is JavaScript-rendered and its text could not be extracted; the Participant Agreement and Rulebook are the operative documents and were read instead.
- Official list of eligible US states/jurisdictions not found in primary sources.
- Whether retail API keys require a separate written approval step beyond in-app key creation is not documented publicly.

## Key question 6: Liquidity provision, market makers, depth and spreads

### Takeaway
Liquidity comes from an application-only Market Maker Program (rebates paid as a share of taker fees to firms with ≥5% maker share in a series), an open per-second Liquidity Incentive Program on designated markets, and taker-volume pools; no firm is publicly named as a Polymarket US market maker, and official docs warn that depth thins or vanishes late in live games.

### Cited Findings
- Three institutional programs: Volume Incentive Program (open; taker volume share), Liquidity Incentive Program (open; "Rewards for placing resting orders, whether they fill or not"; live schedule at polymarket.us/rewards), Market Maker Program (application; contact institutional@qcex.com). Rulebook Ch. 4: Market Makers may receive "reduced fees, and different Position Accountability Levels." — [Incentives Overview](https://docs.polymarket.us/incentives/overview.md), [Market Maker Program](https://docs.polymarket.us/incentives/market-maker.md), [Rulebook Ch.4 (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules03202641475.pdf)
- 2026 Amended Series-Based Market Maker Program (CFTC 40.6(a) filing dated Sept 30, 2026; effective 10 business days after certification, one-year term): rebates to approved Program Participants "based on each Program Participant's share of total maker notional volume within each opted-in Series during each calendar month. Only passive fills count"; "Rebates are paid as a percentage of the Taker Fees charged on a Program Participant's qualifying passive fills tiered by market share and further adjusted by a Volume Credit Multiplier, and Time Weight Multiplier"; eligibility requires ≥5% of total maker notional in the Series that month; eligible categories are sports (by sport or league), cryptocurrency price contracts, and political event contracts; subject to an aggregate monthly cap; paid within 7 business days of month-end; the "Market Share and Multipliers" section is [REDACTED] in the public copy; anti-gaming covers related/coordinated accounts, quote stuffing, layering, spoofing, non-bona-fide quotes. — [CFTC filing rules09302630733 (PDF)](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/orgrules/rules09302630733.pdf)
- Liquidity Incentive Program mechanics: every second a random order-book snapshot is scored; Score = DiscountFactor^(ticks from best price) × size; each side normalized to 1.0 per snapshot if Target Size is met; optional Max Spread (e.g. 3.5¢ from mid) must be met or the second pays nobody; time periods: pre-day (listing to 6 h before), day-of (6 h before to start), live (start to settlement), daily for futures; rewards computed within 5 business days and credited within 2 more; minimum payout $1.00; "No rewards are distributed for canceled or postponed games." Discount factors cited: 0.30 (example), 0.90 (MLB futures). — [Liquidity Incentive Program](https://docs.polymarket.us/incentives/liquidity.md)
- Volume Incentive Program: pools paid on share of taker-side notional, only trades between $0.03 and $0.97 count, minimum $500 notional; example: $100,000 pool per NBA Playoffs moneyline market for in-game trading (live from May 21). — [Volume Incentive Program](https://docs.polymarket.us/incentives/volume.md)
- Press (secondary): FOW reported on March 4, 2026 that Polymarket notified the CFTC of changes to its "Standing Order Incentive Program" aimed at depth on Polymarket US (paywalled) — [FOW](https://www.fow.com/insights/polymarket-amends-incentive-programme-aimed-at-increasing-liquidity); Bloomberg/Cointelegraph reported Polymarket recruiting an in-house market-making desk that may trade against users (date not visible) — [Cointelegraph](https://regional-front.cointelegraph.com/news/polymarket-in-house-market-making-desk-trade-users); Susquehanna is reported as a market maker on Kalshi, not Polymarket US — same source.
- Official depth warning: liquidity "can become thin—or even disappear entirely" in live sports "Late in the 4th quarter or final minutes, Overtime, Right after a major play ... Right before the match ends"; docs recommend splitting a $100 order into four $25 clips. — [Liquidity](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/advanced/liquidity.md), [Price Impact](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/advanced/price-impact.md)
- Observed book (boxing moneyline, 2026-10-24, pre-event, 1¢ tick): bids 0.71 × 9,177 and 0.70 × 15,173; offers 0.72 × 7 (!), 0.74 × 24,110, 0.76 × 23; bidDepth 7 / askDepth 8 levels; i.e. a 1¢ displayed spread with only 7 contracts at the touch and the real size 2¢ back. — [gateway book](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/markets/aec-boxing-canalv-chrmbi-2026-10-31-canalv-chrmbi/book)
- Secondary depth datapoint: River's July 28, 2026 capture found Polymarket US's top ask one cent above Kalshi's and a 50,000-contract sweep moving the price 3.62¢ above the displayed ask. — [River Markets](https://www.rivermarkets.com/insights/polymarket-us-fees.html)
- Pre-clarification periods may see reduced liquidity and wider spreads; resting orders may be cancelled by the exchange. — [Market Clarification](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/markets/clarifications.md)

### Inferences
- Incentive-eligible makers are paid per second for resting near the touch regardless of fills, which rationally produces small "bait" size at the best price backed by large size one or two ticks behind (as observed); an IOC at the displayed ask therefore frequently fills only a few contracts or at the next level.
- Makers with no rebate below $10M/month and taker fees funding the MM program imply the professional makers' economics come from spread plus program rebates; a $135 account cannot access either.

### Gaps
- No public list of designated market makers on Polymarket US; MM program tier percentages are redacted.
- No systematic spread/depth statistics for low-attention markets were found; only anecdotal captures.

## Key question 7: Funding mechanics and yield

### Takeaway
Deposits via debit card, ACH (Aeropay), Apple Pay, PayPal, Venmo, USDC (iPhone) or wire, with 3–4 business days to clear (instant buying power meanwhile); withdrawals are free but must go back to the original funding source, FIFO, after clearing; no document found states that idle cash or held positions earn any interest.

### Cited Findings
- Deposit methods and limits: Debit card $50,000/day, 3–4 business days; ACH $50,000/day, 3–4 business days (ACH also capped $25,000/transaction and $2,000,000 per rolling 60 days, via Aeropay); wire $5,000 minimum, no max, 1 business day; "Deposits other than wire transfers may be credited with instant buying power while the deposit is processing"; deposits must come from accounts in the user's own name. — [Deposit Methods](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/deposits/deposit-methods/overview.md), [Bank Transfer (ACH)](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/deposits/deposit-methods/bank-transfer.md)
- Withdrawals: "Withdraw your cash balance securely and for free"; debit card and ACH 3–4 business days; wire 1 business day but only via support; USDC to a crypto wallet on iPhone where available; "Withdrawals must return to the original funding source"; FIFO across deposit methods; deposits must fully clear first; promotional credits are not withdrawable. — [Withdraw Funds](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/deposits/withdraw-funds/overview.md), [Withdrawal Rules](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/deposits/withdraw-funds/rules.md), [Check Your Balance](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/get-started/cash.md)
- Custody: "Your funds are kept in a dedicated customer account that is separate from Polymarket US's operating funds." — [Is my money safe?](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/faq/money-safety.md)
- Collateral economics while holding: longs pay the price up front; shorts receive proceeds but lock $1.00 per contract; "There is no collateral release from offsets or favorable price moves" (except documented mutually-exclusive/directional collateral return structures). — [Collateral and Margin](https://docs.polymarket.us/market-structure/collateral-and-margin.md)
- Participant Agreement §IV: participant pays fees "as specified from time to time on PMUS's websites"; the only "interest" in the agreement is the security interest pledged to Polymarket Clearing over collateral; no provision for interest paid to participants. — [Participant Agreement (PDF)](https://polymarketexchange.com/files/legal/latest/participant-agreement)
- Incentive credits exist (Deposit Incentive Program, Refer-A-Friend, Deposit and Trading Incentive Program; Amended Deposit Incentive Program notice dated Oct 21, 2026) but are promotional credits, not interest. — [Incentives Overview](https://docs.polymarket.us/incentives/overview.md), [Notices and Filings](https://docs.polymarket.us/notices-and-filings.md)
- Ledger access (institutional API): balance ledger entries typed as deposits, withdrawals, fills, fees, adjustments; historical floor 2026-05-01. — [Positions & Risk](https://docs.polymarket.us/trader-guide/positions-risk.md)
- Secondary (GLOBAL, not US): analyses of Polymarket's pUSD float and Treasury yield capture concern the global venue and are models, not disclosures. — [Imajinl Substack](https://imajinl.substack.com/p/monetizing-prediction-markets)
- Secondary (US): Action Network lists ACH, debit, wire and crypto withdrawals with fiat 1–4 business days — [Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/prediction-markets/polymarket)

### Inferences
- The all-in cost of capital for a small sports bot is therefore the taker fee plus the opportunity cost of cash earning nothing between settlement cycles; there is no carry to offset a thin edge.
- The "withdraw to original source, FIFO" rule and 3–4 day clearing mean profits cannot be swept out quickly; plan bankroll sizing accordingly.

### Gaps
- No primary-source statement either way on interest on idle balances at Polymarket US (searched docs, Participant Agreement, FAQs); absence of any mention plus the free-withdrawal/segregated-account language suggests none is paid, but this is unconfirmed.
- Whether USDC deposits/withdrawals carry network fees was not documented.
