Social card templates

Rendered at 2× and posted as images. One game, one card, one link. Nothing on these templates tells anyone what to do.

Forbidden on every template: bet, wager, lock, best bet, play, hammer, smash, unit, and any figure that implies a return. The only verbs are projects, reads, moved and grades. A template that cannot be filled without one of the forbidden words is a broken template.
